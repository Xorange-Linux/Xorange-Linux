## Overview

**Xorange-Linux** (Codename: **Banana**) is an independent, lightweight operating system designed to run fast especially on older or low-spec laptops. More like ZorinOS but.. it was smoother(i think) and will last forever due to its rolling release

Originally started on a Debian Testing base, Xorange-Linux has been completely rebuilt on **Arch Linux** to take advantage of rolling-release packages, a minimal core footprint, and full control over every component installed on the system.

> [!NOTE]
> **Not hardware tested!!** for this reason, the iso will not release because its hard to make, turning a qcow2 img to iso is hard

## Minimum and Recommended System Requirements

| Component | Minimum Specification | Recommended Specification | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Architecture** | x86_64 (64-bit) | only x86_64 (64-bit) | 64-bit processor required |
| **CPU** | 1 GHz (1 Core) | Dual-Core 1.8 GHz+ | Built & tested on Intel Core i3-3217U |
| **RAM** | 700 MiB | 2 GiB or more | Low idle memory usage out of the box |
| **Storage** | 15 GiB | 25 GiB or more | SSD recommended for faster boot & updates |
| **Installing Media** | USB Drive (2 GiB+) | USB 3.0 Drive (2 GiB+) | Because recent iso take 1.6gb so |

## Installation Guide

|Bootable creator app | Rating(in dev opinion) | Useful for |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Ventoy | #### | Useful for managing many iso at once |
| Rufus | #### | For reviving old tech |
| Balena Etcher | ##### | same as Rufus

**Open one of the installer, if rufus,select you prefered drive, choose your partition type,(GPT mean UEFI and CSM mean Legacy bios, to check your bios type, reboot your pc/laptop/system, spam f2 and go to boot tab and change the thing as you like) and choose your iso and flash and do the thing below, Next is Ventoy(the china rufus but better) Open the installer, select your drive and flash ventoy on it( you can change the partition type by go to the thing on the top left and choose bios and choose your bios type) and after that, put your iso in the Ventoy disk, this time, do the same thing but it will send you to some bootloader thing and choose your iso and enter and boot and do the thing below, Last is Balena Etcher, put your iso and select your drive and flash, after that, just the the thing below like rufus**

**So after flashing, restart the system and press f12 or esc(depending on your brand) and boot the usb and enter and on the login screen, Username:xorange   Pass:x11 and open the installer and put your info,disk and done and reboot**
